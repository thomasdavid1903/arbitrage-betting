How this differs from arbitrage

An arbitrage needs two bookmakers wrong at once, which is why the same scan finds a handful. A value bet needs one: take Pinnacle’s prices as the best available estimate of what will happen, strip its margin out, and look for a soft book offering more than that. You carry the risk — most of these lose — but the expectation is positive and the opportunities are an order of magnitude more common. Nothing here suggests placing anything.
